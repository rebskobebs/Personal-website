Who I am

About

I'm a BSFS student at Georgetown University in Qatar, majoring in International Economics with minors in Mathematics and Philosophy.

I am currently a CRF Summer Research Fellow at Georgetown's Center for Research and Fellowships, working on "Remittance and Household Consumption in Nepal" with Professor Lamis Kattan. As a Finance Student Assistant in the GUQ Finance Office, I contribute to operational efficiency through Excel-based dashboards, automated procurement workflows, and process documentation — guided by Director of Strategy and Process Improvement, Chris Barrington.

I am interested in academic research and philosophical inquiry to understand the complex reality we live in. I specialize in quantitative and qualitative research practices, and am skilled in R Programming, Stata, Atlas.ti, and Excel. My academic and professional pursuits reflect my commitment to research, data analysis, and understanding global and local socio-economic issues.

I have done fieldwork in Kathmandu through 22 semi-structured interviews with voters, political leaders, and civil society members. I have also written on labor diplomacy and the kafala system, and spent three years contributing to Nepal's debate community as a trainer, organizer, and equity advocate.
